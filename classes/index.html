Move your way

Classes with good energy.

Explore formats that challenge your body, clear your mind, and make you want to come back for more.

01

Indoor Cycle

Turn up the music and ride at your own level. Each class pairs coached intervals with a motivating room that helps you build strength, stamina, and confidence one ride at a time.

02

Barre

Small, intentional movements meet full-body strength work. Expect balance, core engagement, and the kind of focused effort that leaves you feeling capable and connected.

03

Yoga + Stretch

Find mobility, space, and a steadier rhythm. These classes welcome beginners and experienced practitioners with a practice built around mindful movement.

04

Spin & Sculpt

Start with heart-pumping bike intervals, then move to the mat for weights, bodyweight training, and core work. A strong, satisfying blend of cardio and conditioning.
